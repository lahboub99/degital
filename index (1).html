    What the metrics mean

    • Cash flow: money left each month after all expenses and the mortgage.
    • NOI: income after vacancy and operating costs, before mortgage payments.
    • Cap rate: NOI divided by purchase price. A financing-free yardstick of yield.
    • Cash-on-cash: annual cash flow divided by the cash you put in.
    • DSCR: NOI divided by yearly mortgage payments. Lenders often want 1.25x or more.
    • Break-even occupancy: share of the year the property must be rented to cover costs and the mortgage.
    • Gross yield: scheduled yearly income divided by purchase price.
    • Yield on cost: NOI divided by total project cost (price, closing, renovation, one-time costs).

    Method

    • Loan = price minus down payment. Closing, renovation and one-time costs are paid in cash.
    • Vacancy reduces gross income. Maintenance, management and CapEx reserve are percentages of effective income. Tax, insurance, utilities, HOA and other costs are fixed amounts.
    • NOI excludes mortgage payments. Payment = L r / (1 - (1+r)^-n), or L/n at 0% interest.
    • All results are estimates from your inputs, not verified facts. Fields left blank are flagged; zero values are listed in the assumptions summary.
